GIS Operations Reference

GeometryOps.jl · JuliaGeo · 2026
Set operations
01
Intersection
The region shared by both geometries. GO.intersection(a, b)
02
Union
The combined footprint of A and B. GO.union(a, b)
03
Difference
A with B's overlap removed. GO.difference(a, b)
04
Symmetric Difference
Parts exclusive to each polygon — XOR. Neither share. GO.symdiff(a, b)
Spatial operations
05
Buffer
Expands geometry outward by distance d. Rounds corners at vertices.
06
Convex Hull
Smallest convex polygon enclosing all input points. GO.convex_hull(pts)
07
Simplification
Removes redundant vertices within tolerance ε. Douglas-Peucker algorithm. GO.simplify(geom, ε)
08
Segmentize
Adds intermediate vertices so no edge exceeds max_d. Required before reprojection. GO.segmentize(geom, max_d)
Measurement
09
Centroid
Area-weighted center of mass. Not guaranteed inside non-convex polygons. GO.centroid(geom)
10
Signed Area
Sign encodes ring orientation. Used to detect holes vs exteriors. GO.signed_area(ring)
11
Distance
Closest approach between two geometries. Zero when they touch or overlap. GO.distance(a, b)
12
Point-in-Polygon
Cast a ray rightward. Odd crossing count → inside. Even → outside. GO.contains(poly, pt)
Predicates
13
Contains / Within
Every point of B lies inside A. Inverse: B is within A. GO.contains(a, b)
14
Intersects / Disjoint
Geometries share at least one point. Opposite is disjoint. GO.intersects(a, b)
15
Touches
Geometries share only boundary — interiors are disjoint. GO.touches(a, b)
16
Winding Order
CCW exterior rings, CW holes. GeoJSON mandates this; many formats do not enforce it. GO.fix(geom)
Spherical geometry
17
Great Circle Path
The shortest path between two points on a sphere. Curves visibly when projected flat — straight-line shortcuts cut through the Earth. GO.segmentize densifies edges along great circles before reprojection.
18
Geodesic Distance
True arc length on the ellipsoid surface: d = R·θ where θ is the central angle. Chord length (dashed) is always shorter and wrong for GIS. Computed via Haversine or Vincenty. GO.distance(P, Q)
19
Reprojection
Transforms coordinates from one CRS to another. Shape distorts based on projection. GO.reproject(geom, crs)
20
Triangulation
Decomposes polygon into non-overlapping triangles. Enables rendering and interpolation. GO.triangulate(poly)
Decomposition & interpolation
21
Delaunay Triangulation
Triangulation maximising minimum angles. No point lies inside any triangle's circumcircle. GO.triangulate(pts, :delaunay)
22
Voronoi Diagram
Partitions space so each cell contains every point closest to its seed. Dual of Delaunay. GO.voronoi(pts)
23
Barycentric Coordinates
Weights (λ₁,λ₂,λ₃) expressing P as a blend of triangle vertices. Used for interpolation. GO.barycentric_coordinates(tri, pt)
